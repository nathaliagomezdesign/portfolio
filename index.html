Realidad Au Pair

Designing a digital ecosystem for a structured and supported au pair experience

Role: Solo Product Designer · UX/UI

Timeline: 12 weeks

Tools: Figma · Optimal Workshop · Canva · NotebookLM

Language: Spanish

Project type: Individual academic project developed as part of my UX/UI Master's degree

The challenge

Au pairs sustain households without adequate support systems:

  • Complex Scope: They manage shifting responsibilities in ambiguous, emotionally demanding contexts
  • Lack of Support: They operate without clear frameworks or professional guidance
  • Core Problem: The underlying issue isn't the workload itself — it's the total lack of structure

The solution

A support system designed to turn an unstructured, improvisational journey into a guided experience by empowering au pairs to:

  • Define their role: Gain clarity on daily responsibilities and expectations
  • Set boundaries: Establish clear, healthy limits within the host family context
  • Foster growth: Turn their day-to-day experience into conscious personal and professional learning

User Research & Key Insights

Data Source & Community Engagement

To ground this project in authentic user needs, I built and managed the Instagram community (@realidadaupair) from scratch, executing the content strategy, visual identity, and audience engagement. Growing this platform gave me direct access to real au pair stories, ongoing qualitative feedback, and an immediate recruitment pool to gather 50 survey responses and conduct real-user testing.

Quantitative Insights (Survey with 50 au pairs)

  • Emotional Strain: Most active au pairs rate the emotional impact of their experience between 3 and 5 out of 5
  • Community & Safety Needs: High demand for local community connection and immediate assistance features (emergency options, situation translators)

Key Insight: The strongest need wasn't access to more resources, but having a clearer way to organize and manage everyday challenges

Qualitative Insights (In-depth Interviews with 4 users)

  • Initial Friction: Agencies provide strong initial matching support but lack continuous operational backing
  • Communication Gaps: Verbal instructions and ambiguous house rules lead to friction over time
  • Retrospective Feedback: Au pairs consistently agree that clear initial guidelines and boundary-setting strategies would have significantly improved their stay

Key Insight: Au pairs need structured tools to manage daily childcare tasks while navigating delicate emotional boundaries with host families

Information Architecture & Feature Prioritization

Core Navigation Structure

  • Task & Schedule Planner: Daily schedule views, child-specific agendas, and task reminders
  • Emotional Wellbeing: Daily mood check-ins, emotional history tracking, and stress metrics
  • Help & Guidance: Emergency action flows, communication templates for family discussions, and situation resolution
  • Resources & Community: Practical care tips, activity ideas, and local au pair networking

These four core areas were prioritized to directly address the main pain points uncovered in research: lack of structure, emotional isolation, unclear boundaries, and limited peer support.

Concept Testing & Design Iterations

Tested the prototype with 6 users through task-based usability sessions

  • Usability Sessions & Methodology: Tested the prototype with 6 active au pairs through task-based usability sessions to validate core navigation, feature clarity, and emotional relevance before finalizing high-fidelity screens
  • Core User Tasks Tested: Locate where to log daily mood, add a new task to the daily schedule, find immediate support when feeling overwhelmed or isolated, search for childcare activity ideas and resources

Key Findings & MVP Strategy Shift

  • Task vs. Static Content: Users prioritized instant problem-solving tools and daily task planning over static general guides
  • MVP Refinement: Streamlined the product scope to focus strictly on three high-value areas: daily task organization, immediate situation support, and emotional tracking

Usability Refinements

  • Emotional Tracking Screen: Replaced confusing status labels with a clear, direct Call-to-Action (“Add an emotion for today”) and streamlined statistical widgets
  • Home Dashboard: Reorganized vertical spacing, corrected color contrast issues, and introduced direct entry points for local community features
  • Contextual Support: Replaced static help texts with active assistance components (guided prompts and immediate action flows)

  • Balanced spacing
  • Colors and contrast
  • Clear text hierarchy
  • “Go to the Au Pair guide” button
  • Access to the community

BEFORE:

  • Static content
  • CTA button

AFTER:

  • Contextual help
  • Optimized button

Key Takeaways

  • More information wasn't the solution: Research showed that au pairs already had access to large amounts of information. The challenge was finding the right resource when they needed it
  • Prioritization shaped the MVP: Instead of trying to solve every pain point, we prioritized the Planner as the core feature based on user needs and feasibility
  • Testing challenged my assumptions: Some features and navigation patterns that seemed intuitive during the design phase were not immediately understood by users, leading to changes in the information architecture

Next Steps

  • A/B Testing: Validate conversion rate and usability for the new quick-help flows
  • Micro-interactions: Polish haptic feedback animations for daily mood tracking to boost engagement
  • Community Discovery: Design a local networking tool enabling au pairs to connect with nearby peers, directly addressing the pain point of loneliness

Angel Fashion

Improving the digital experience of a beauty & wellness center

Role: Solo Product Designer · UX/UI

Timeline: 4 weeks

Tools: Figma · Lovable · Leveraged AI to generate initial content drafts, then refined them for tone, accuracy, and design direction

Language: Italian

Project type: UX/UI Case Study · Website Redesign

The challenge & Impact

Angel Fashion is a beauty salon specializing in aesthetic treatments, nail care, and personal body care

  • Broken UX & Low Trust: Low-contrast text, missing CTAs, duplicate navigation, and poor visual credibility undermined the salon's high-end brand positioning
  • Conversion Obstacles: Key information like pricing and services was hard to find, driving high bounce rates and lost booking opportunities
  • Operational Friction: Cognitive overload forced first-time visitors to call directly, burdening staff with basic administrative inquiries

The solution

Redesign the website into an intuitive, high-converting storefront that builds instant trust and streamlines bookings

  • Trust & Authenticity First: Replacing generic stock photos with real salon photography, and swapping unverified text quotes for transparent Google Review integrations
  • Information Architecture Cleanup: Merging duplicate navigation bars into a single, predictable structure centered around core user tasks
  • Conversion & Accessibility: Restructure the visual hierarchy with WCAG-compliant contrast, clean UI elements, and persistent CTAs across all devices to streamline bookings

UX Audit & Key Insights

To identify the root causes of bounce rates and friction, I conducted a heuristic audit of the current platform. The findings were mapped based on User Impact vs. Business Impact to prioritize solutions

  • Generic Stock Images Destroy Credibility: Generic stock photos replaced real work and staff, creating user doubt and friction during decision-making
  • Text Density & Unprofessional Tone: AI-generated text blocks with system emojis and low contrast caused severe cognitive overload and poor visual accessibility
  • Navigational Friction & Unusable CTAs: Stacked navigation menus and broken CTAs created severe navigation friction, causing users to drop off before booking
  • Unverified Social Proof (“Dicono di Noi”): Unverified, text-only testimonials without names or photos raised user skepticism instead of building credibility

IA & Design Direction

Before jumping into high-fidelity visuals, I restructured the site hierarchy to guide users smoothly through their decision-making process

  • Simplified Navigation: Combined the stacked header menus into a clean, single-tier bar
  • Clear Service Categorization: Grouped scattered treatments into broad, recognizable categories
  • Human & Welcoming Voice: Adapted content to speak directly to self-care, peace of mind, and time efficiency — addressing the core emotional reasons people book salon appointments
  • Elevated Visual Identity: Established a warm, minimal color palette with accessible contrast ratios, paired with clean iconography to replace emojis
  • Mobile-First Accessibility: Optimized touch targets, thumb-zone CTA placements, and scannable service cards for on-the-go browsing

Omnichannel Alignment: Social Media & Brand

Brand Consistency & Audience Conversion

Parallel to the web redesign, I managed Angel Fashion's visual identity and social media ecosystem. By creating a unified design language across Instagram, Google Business Profiles, and the web platform, I aligned brand touchpoints to tell an authentic story — using strategic content to drive organic traffic directly into the high-converting web booking path.

The Redesign

  • Desktop Layout: Hero section with high-contrast typography, clear 3×2 treatment grid, verified Google Reviews section, and distinct location cards
  • Mobile Experience: Optimized single-column layout, dark-mode overlays for high contrast, collapsible hub cards for physical locations (Bologna / Casalecchio di Reno), and a clean hamburger menu overlay

Key Design Decisions

Header & Navigation Architecture

  • Before: Stacked, chaotic double header with duplicate categories directly above, creating severe visual noise and confusing users
  • After: Unified single-tier navigation paired with a persistent, high-contrast gold CTA button (Prenota ora)

Visual Hierarchy & Content Scannability

  • Before: Overwhelming SEO text blocks crammed with system emojis and low-contrast typography, giving an unprofessional impression that damaged brand credibility
  • After: Restructured content using a clean typography scale, generous white space, custom icons, and scannable treatment cards that communicate quality at a glance

Key decisions: Replaced text-heavy paragraphs with structured cards featuring high-end photography and bite-sized descriptions, and replaced system emojis with refined brand icons and a clear typography hierarchy to elevate brand trust

Social Proof & Trust Indicators (“Dicono di noi”)

  • Before: Plain-text, unverified testimonials without star ratings, reviewer sources, or credibility indicators, making them appear fabricated
  • After: Integrated a verified Google Reviews section displaying actual customer feedback alongside an official Google Rating to validate trust immediately before the booking section

Multi-Location Access & Mobile Experience

  • Before: Unoptimized mobile layout where users had to wade through endless text and generic stock photos to find physical locations or contact details
  • After: Grouped location cards into regional hubs (Bologna & Casalecchio di Reno) and introduced a clean mobile hamburger menu overlay, drastically reducing mobile scroll length and cognitive load

Next Steps & Takeaways

  • Designing for Multi-Location Businesses: Structuring navigation for multiple physical salons requires balancing local SEO needs with clean, uncluttered mobile UX
  • The Power of Verified Proof: Integrating real photos and verified Google ratings directly into key decision points is as critical to conversion as clean visual design
  • End-to-End Brand Cohesion: Connecting social media content strategy with UI design creates a unified experience that reinforces customer trust across all digital touchpoints
